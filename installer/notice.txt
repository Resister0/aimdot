Aimdot - a very light crosshair overlay
https://sonela.app/aimdot

WHAT IT INSTALLS
Aimdot installs for you only (no administrator rights) into your user folder (AppData\Local\Aimdot), with a Start menu entry and an uninstaller in Windows Settings > Apps. Your settings and crosshair images live in AppData\Roaming\Aimdot.

PRIVACY
Aimdot keeps your settings on this PC. The only thing it does by itself is check sonela.app every few hours for a newer version; that check contains Aimdot's version number and your system type (Windows or Linux). No ID and nothing about you.

FAIR PLAY
Some competitive games and anti-cheat systems don't allow overlays or crosshair tools. Check the game's rules before you use it.
